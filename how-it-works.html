FOR FARMERS

Your produce moves
through a clear process.

You focus on farming. AgroLink handles the process between your farm and the businesses that need your produce.

01

Partner with AgroLink

Register your farm and tell us what produce you have available.

02

Schedule collection

When your produce is ready, arrange a suitable collection with AgroLink.

03

We collect & verify

Our operations team collects the produce and checks its condition before storage.

04

We sell to businesses

Your produce becomes part of our wholesale supply network for business buyers.

WHAT HAPPENS AT AGROLINK

We handle the middle.

Once produce enters our network, our operations team manages the movement from farm to buyer.

01

Collection

Produce is collected from partner farms according to scheduled pickups.

02

Verification

Produce is checked before entering the warehouse supply chain.

03

Storage

Produce is organized and stored until it is matched with business demand.

04

Distribution

Orders are prepared and moved toward the businesses that purchased them.
