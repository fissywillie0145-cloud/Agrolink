ABOUT AGROLINK

Building the network behind agriculture.

AgroLink is building a structured agricultural supply network that connects farmers, operations and businesses so produce can move from farms to the people and businesses that need it.

One network. From farm to business.
OUR STORY

Agriculture produces.
AgroLink connects.

Agriculture is one of the most important parts of Nigeria's economy, yet moving agricultural produce from farms to businesses can still be difficult, fragmented and unpredictable.

Farmers may have produce ready for sale without having reliable access to buyers. At the same time, traders, wholesalers and businesses may need large quantities of produce but struggle to find a dependable supply.

AgroLink exists to connect these two sides through an organized supply network. Rather than leaving every farmer and buyer to figure out the process independently, AgroLink coordinates the middle — collection, verification, storage, marketing and distribution.

The goal is simple: make agricultural trade more organized, more accessible and easier for businesses to participate in.

THE PROBLEM WE SEE

Good produce should not struggle to find a market.

The agricultural supply chain involves many moving parts. Without coordination, opportunities can be lost on both sides of the market.

01

Farmers need reliable markets

Farmers invest time and resources into producing crops. Having a dependable route to market can make the selling process more predictable.

02

Businesses need dependable supply

Wholesalers, merchants and other businesses need access to agricultural products in quantities they can actually plan around.

03

The middle needs structure

Collection, storage, verification and distribution require coordination. This is where AgroLink operates.

OUR MISSION

To create a better connection between farms and business.

We are building an agricultural network where farmers can participate in a structured route to market and businesses can access agricultural produce through a more organized supply system.
